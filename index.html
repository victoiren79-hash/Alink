Our Story

A Tradition of Authenticity

Alink Restaurant brings generations of culinary tradition from China and Korea to the heart of Kigali.

Founded with a passion for authentic flavors, we prepare every dish using traditional techniques and the freshest ingredients. Our vegetables and tofu come directly from our owner's farm, ensuring quality and sustainability.

Our restaurant offers a calm, welcoming atmosphere where families, friends, and business colleagues can enjoy memorable dining experiences.
